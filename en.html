Services

Reinforcement for every component

From the base slab to the last column: we fix the reinforcement exactly as the structural engineer planned it.

  1. Area reinforcement for base slabs and underground car parks: bars and mesh in bottom and top layer, with spacers and support chairs.

  2. Floor reinforcement in residential and commercial buildings – in step with the formwork cycle, so concreting stays on schedule.

  3. Wall reinforcement, including great heights – fixed safely from scissor lifts, with starter bars and additional reinforcement.

  4. Reinforcement cages for columns: longitudinal bars and links, cleanly tied and set to height.

  5. Beams and downstand beams with dense reinforcement – links, longitudinal bars and connections in the right order.

  6. Pad and strip foundations in the excavation pit – the first steel of your building.

How we work

From the drawing to the slab

Scroll on – this is how a reinforced slab comes together, layer by layer.

01 / 05

Check the drawings

We read the reinforcement drawings and bar schedule and clarify questions with site management before the first bar is laid.

02 / 05

Spacers & bottom layer

Spacers secure the concrete cover. On top we lay the bottom layer in both directions.

03 / 05

Support chairs & top layer

Support chairs hold the top layer at height – then the second reinforcement layer follows.

04 / 05

Tie every crossing

Crossings are secured with tie wire so nothing shifts when the concrete is poured.

05 / 05

Inspection – ready to pour

Joint check with site management – then the area is free for concreting.

What does KRONOS BAU GmbH do?

We are a construction company that carries out reinforcement works only: we place and tie reinforcing steel and mesh to the reinforcement drawings – for slabs, floors, walls, columns, beams and foundations.

Who do you work for?

For construction companies, general contractors and shell-construction firms in building and civil engineering that need their own experienced crew for the reinforcement.

Where do you work?

On construction sites in Germany. Tell us the location of your site and the time frame – we will quickly tell you whether we can schedule it.

What do you need for a quote?

Ideally the reinforcement drawings and the bar schedule as PDF, plus location, preferred time frame and – if known – the tonnage. With this we calculate your quote.

Do you also supply the reinforcing steel?

Our core business is placing and tying the reinforcement. How the material is provided is agreed together in the quote.

Do you also work on tall walls?

Yes. We fix wall reinforcement at height from scissor lifts and scaffolding – with personal protective equipment and following the site rules.
